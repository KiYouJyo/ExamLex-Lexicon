A1 Pilot SQLite - attribution and scope

This is a small 120-ish-word A1 development pilot, NOT the complete Oxford A1 word list.
The word/POS manifest is a short example subset. No Oxford dictionary definitions are included.

Lexical content in the pilot was imported from Simple English Wiktionary contributors
via Kaikki.org / Wiktextract:
Source https://kaikki.org/simplewiktionary/
Wiki https://simple.wiktionary.org/
JSONL https://kaikki.org/simplewiktionary/raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl.gz
Wiktionary textual contributions are available under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike licensing; review exact wiki and content license notices
and preserve required attribution and ShareAlike obligations when redistributing.
Wiktextract extractor: Tatu Ylonen, https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

Oxford 3000 is referenced only for learning level scope. Oxford's wordlist
compilation and dictionary content are not licensed for redistribution here.
https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/terms-and-conditions

This dataset is built from word/POS screening, not A1-sense-specific annotation;
some uncommon senses may be above A1. Check a1-import-report.json for coverage.
